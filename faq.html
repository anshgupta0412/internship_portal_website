Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I apply for more than one internship?

A: Yes, feel free to apply for multiple opportunities based on your skills and interests.

Q: Are these internships paid?

A: Both paid and unpaid internships are listed. You can filter by payment method on the portal.

Q: What if I don’t receive a response?

A: If you don’t hear back within 10 days, consider applying for more internships. We update listings regularly.

Q: Do I get a certificate after the internship?

A: Yes, most companies offer completion certificates. Check the internship details to confirm.
